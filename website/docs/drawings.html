User manual / Drawings

Drawings

RazorCAD makes 2D engineering drawings of parts, saved as .rdrw files and opened in their own tab.

Starting a drawing

  • Start window ▸ New Drawing, or File ▸ New Drawing: an empty A3 sheet.
  • File ▸ Make Drawing from Part (in a part), or Make Drawing from Assembly (in an assembly): places the standard three views at a scale that fits.
  • A drawing can show assemblies as well as parts: every component is drawn where it sits, through its sub-assemblies, in the configurations shown; suppressed and hidden components are left out. Big assemblies (more than about 200 solids) are drawn fast from the parts' display meshes: lines, circles and arcs as exact as the 3D view's, and where an edge turns hidden to about two thousandths of the view's size. A site layout of thousands of solids then takes seconds a view instead of many minutes. In a view's properties, Hidden lines chooses Automatic, Exact or Fast; section and flat-pattern views are always exact.

Drawings are opened, saved and listed in the recent files like parts.

The sheet and its template

The default template is A3 with an ISO 5457 frame (zones and centring marks) and an ISO 7200-style title block: title, drawing number, material, scale, size, units, projection symbol, drawn / checked / approved, date, mass, company, revision, sheet n of m and general tolerances. Sheet sizes A0 to A4, landscape or portrait.

Views are hidden-line projections computed by the geometry kernel.

View properties

Click a view to open its panel:

  • Orientation, changeable after placing (also to and from Flat Pattern; projected and section views made from it are removed when it becomes a flat pattern, and Ctrl+Z brings them back).
  • Display style: hidden lines removed, hidden lines visible, or wireframe; tangent edges on or off.
  • Sheet scale or a custom scale; labels.
  • Rotate View: −90°, +90°, 180° and 0° buttons and an angle box (degrees, counter-clockwise). Section lines, detail circles and annotations turn with the view.

Aligned views slide only along their alignment. Deleting a view deletes the views made from it.

Flat pattern views

A flat pattern view shows a sheet metal body unfolded with its outline and holes, bend lines (chain lines) with notes (UP / DOWN, angle, radius), optional bend region lines, and its flat size, thickness, number of bends and cut length underneath. It can be dimensioned (bend lines too) and exported to PDF. If the part has several sheet metal bodies, pick the body in the view properties.

Sheet tab

  • Sheet Properties: size, orientation, scale, first or third angle.
  • Title Block (Drawing Properties): title block fields, text height and decimals.
  • Add Sheet: drawings can have several sheets, shown as tabs under the sheet.
  • Export PDF: vector PDF, one page per sheet.

Views follow the part

Views and dimensions follow changes to the part, whether it is open in another tab or read from its file. A copy of the part is kept in the drawing so it still opens if the part file is missing.
